THE PLAYBOOK

Three ways to play.

01

Player Guess

You get five clues, from broad to specific, and one guess per clue. A wrong guess or skip unlocks the next clue. Score 5 points for solving clue one, down to 1 point on clue five. Five misses end the game. Names ignore spacing, punctuation, and capitalization; accepted nicknames are set by the owner.

02

Stat Ladder

Answer five questions from easy to hard. Each gets one guess. Rungs are worth 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5 points: 15 total. A miss or skip earns zero, but you keep climbing. Every question names its sport, season, and statistic.

03

CrossSport

Fill the mini crossword with sports names and phrases. Tap a clue to pick a word. Tap an intersection again to switch direction. Use arrow keys on a keyboard. Checks flag incorrect filled letters; reveal fills one letter. Your shared result includes both. Spaces and punctuation are omitted from grid answers.

New games arrive at midnight Eastern. A completion streak counts consecutive dates you finish a game, regardless of score. Each game has its own streak. Practice and owner previews never count. Progress stays in this browser; clearing browser storage removes it.
